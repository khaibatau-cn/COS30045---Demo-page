OUR QUESTION

How does TV energy consumption vary?

We explore how labelled annual energy consumption varies with screen size and screen technology. The charts also describe technology counts and the distribution of energy star ratings.

The aim is to ask useful questions of the data and explain what the results do—and do not—show.

FROM SOURCE TO SCREEN

A workflow you can follow.

  1. Select the relevant fields.

    Keep brand, model, screen size, screen area, screen technology, average power, star rating, and annual energy from the 32-column source.

  2. Standardise brand labels.

    Trim outer spaces and convert labels to uppercase with upperCase(strip($Brand_Reg$)). The number of distinct labels falls from 96 to 81.

  3. Add a familiar measurement.

    Divide screen size in centimetres by 2.54 to create ScreenSizeInches. Keep the original centimetre measurement too.

  4. Validate and export.

    Keep all 4,724 rows, rename the final nine columns, and export the cleaned CSV. Profiling and chart summaries stay on side branches in KNIME.

  5. Explore in context.

    Use five charts to compare counts, relationships, medians, spread, and outliers. Website filters recalculate the charts for the selected records.

READ THE DATA FAIRLY

What to keep in mind.

  • Record counts are not sales figures or necessarily unique TV models.
  • Technology groups contain different screen sizes. Their medians do not isolate a technology's effect on energy use.
  • Three source model values contain the literal placeholder “NA”; they are retained.
  • Different names such as SAMSUNG and SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS remain separate.
  • The file supplied for this project is tv_2026_02_15.csv. Its filename is not proof of a publication date. Original publisher information has not been independently verified.
