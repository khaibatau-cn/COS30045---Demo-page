START WITH THE BASICS

Know what you're comparing.

A few useful distinctions before you explore.

01 / POWER

Watts describe power.

Power tells you the rate at which an appliance uses energy. How long you run it matters too.

02 / ENERGY

kWh adds time.

Kilowatt-hours describe energy consumed over time. Our TV charts use the dataset's labelled annual kWh.

03 / CONTEXT

Size is part of the picture.

Compare screen size alongside energy consumption. A technology's median alone does not tell you its efficiency.
